What it does

Takes photos and stamps them with your place name, address, coordinates, map, date, time, weather and direction, so every picture tells where and when it was taken.

Privacy

Your photos are saved only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and there are no accounts or ads. To fill in the address, map and weather, your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap and Open-Meteo. You can turn off camera and location access anytime in your browser settings.

Accuracy

Location comes from your phone and can be off by several metres, and more indoors. Date and time come from your phone clock and can be edited, so a stamp is a convenience and not legal proof.

Terms of use
