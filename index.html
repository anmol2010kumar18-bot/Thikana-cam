What it does

Takes photos and stamps them with your place name, address, coordinates, map, date, time, weather and direction, so every picture tells where and when it was taken.

Privacy

Your photos are saved only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and there are no accounts or ads. By default each photo file also carries its GPS location and date in its metadata, so gallery apps can show it on a map. You can turn this off in Edit stamp. To fill in the address, map and weather, your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap and Open-Meteo. You can turn off camera and location access anytime in your browser settings.

Accuracy

Location comes from your phone and can be off by several metres, and more indoors. Date and time come from your phone clock and can be edited. Photos made from your gallery use a location you choose by hand, and are marked “Manual location” unless you turn that off. A stamp is a convenience and not legal proof.
